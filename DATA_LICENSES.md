# Data and figure attribution

This repository does not redistribute the full original datasets.

* PBC: Acevedo, A. et al. A dataset for microscopic peripheral blood cell images for development of automatic recognition systems. Mendeley Data v1, DOI [10.17632/snkd93bnjr.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/snkd93bnjr.1), CC BY 4.0. [Associated dataset paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2020.105474).
* BloodMNIST: Yang, J. et al., MedMNIST v2, Scientific Data 10, 41 (2023), [10.1038/s41597-022-01721-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01721-8). [Official data record](https://zenodo.org/records/10519652), CC BY 4.0 for BloodMNIST. BloodMNIST derives from PBC; its images are center-cropped and resized. The executed demo selects six WBC labels and uses its supplied train/validation/test partitions after exact-duplicate screening.
* AML: Matek, C., Schwarz, S., Marr, C., & Spiekermann, K. (2019). A Single-cell Morphological Dataset of Leukocytes from AML Patients and Non-malignant Controls. TCIA. [10.7937/tcia.2019.36f5o9ld](https://doi.org/10.7937/tcia.2019.36f5o9ld), CC BY 3.0. Follow [TCIA data usage requirements](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/aml-cytomorphology_lmu/).
* Raabin: Kouzehkanan, Z. M. et al. A large dataset of white blood cells containing cell locations and types, along with segmented nuclei and cytoplasm. Scientific Reports 12, 1123 (2022), [10.1038/s41598-021-04426-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-04426-x). [Original download portal](https://raabindata.com/). Verify current archive terms before reuse; the website advertises free commercial and noncommercial research access. Ground-truth use additionally cites Tavakoli et al., [10.1038/s41598-021-98599-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-98599-0).

`figures/paper/` contains nine original embedded illustrations extracted unchanged from the supplied manuscript with the requester's authorization. `results/reported/` contains its tabulated summaries. The manuscript is titled **Integrating Stain Information and Cell Morphology Across Multiple Scales for White Blood Cell Classification**, by Md Nafis Azad Nobel, Mainuddin Adel Rafi, Mousumi Akter, Refat Hossain, and Syed Khasim. These assets retain their original rights; no general redistribution license is inferred. Figure 8 includes PBC images and WBCAtt+ reference annotations; cite the PBC source and Tsutsui et al., [WBCAtt+](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2026.104137) when reusing it. The manuscript itself was not uploaded.

Model backbone initialization: official Facebook Research [ConvNeXt](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ConvNeXt), MIT-licensed code. The ConvNeXt-compatible encoder here is independently implemented. Downloads of pretrained weights and torchvision comparator weights remain subject to their upstream terms and training-data terms.
